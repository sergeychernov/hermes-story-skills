# Cultural food stops as content-rich travel beats

Use this reference when the traveller asks where to eat during an active travel story and wants both local culture and strong photo/video material.

## Decision order

1. Resolve the live origin and current local time.
2. Recover **itinerary-wide hard constraints** from the active conversation and durable user context before optimizing for food or content: excluded transport modes, seasickness, accessibility, companions, weather exposure, and walking tolerance. These constraints persist across venue pivots; never reintroduce an excluded ferry, bus, taxi, or other mode merely because it produces better footage.
3. Extract **hard food constraints** before ranking prestige:
   - required dish or style;
   - exclusions such as buffet, display-case, ready-made, alcohol, allergens, or dietary rules;
   - acceptable travel time.
4. Extract the cultural objective: everyday local institution, ceremonial guest destination, market culture, regional specialty, or modern landmark.
5. Extract the content objective: transit journey, street atmosphere, cooking/action shot, table reveal, or skyline/interior.
6. Verify the current menu from the venue's own site/menu when a specific dish is mandatory. A famous venue without the required dish is not a match; say so directly and rerank.
7. Verify current opening status and build the exact compliant public-transport leg. Distinguish metro, tram, suburban rail, and ferry; omit any mode excluded for the traveller or companion.

## Recommendation pattern

Prefer one decisive recommendation plus one fallback, not a long generic restaurant list. State why it satisfies all hard constraints. If the fastest route and the best story route differ, label both:

- **fastest/driest** — simplest live transit;
- **best for the story** — a modestly longer route that adds an authentic narrative beat, such as a market walk, historic tram, visible food preparation, or a ferry crossing **only when water transport is allowed for everyone travelling**.

Never let content value override a food requirement, an itinerary-wide transport or companion constraint, operating hours, weather safety, or a substantially harder trip without saying so. If the cinematic route violates any hard constraint, omit it rather than presenting it as an enticing alternative.

## Content arc

Build a compact shot sequence around the real experience:

1. departure/weather/context;
2. transport transition;
3. neighborhood arrival and exterior;
4. preparation or serving action;
5. close-up of the signature dish;
6. first reaction or quiet closer.

Treat this as capture guidance only. Do not add media to the archive until the user actually sends it. Do not invent a venue's local reputation, founding date, signature dish, or menu availability; source those claims or phrase them conservatively.

## Practical output

Give the traveller:

- exact stop/pier and direction;
- exit stop and stop count when available;
- final walking time;
- tappable live route;
- the local-language dish/order phrase when useful;
- 3–6 shot prompts, only when content creation is part of the request.
